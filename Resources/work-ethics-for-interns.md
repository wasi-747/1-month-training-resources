## 1. Professional Behavior
* **Polite & Respectful Interaction**: Be polite, respectful, and professional with everyone.
* **Equal Treatment**: Treat all team members equally and respect others' opinions.
* **Constructive Feedback**: Accept feedback positively and communicate disagreements professionally.
* **Transparency**: Be honest and transparent about your progress, mistakes, and blockers.
* **Flat Hierarchy & Team Notification**: Unlike traditional companies, we don't operate on a senior/junior hierarchy. If you're taking a break, making a decision, or sharing an update, please notify your entire team rather than just senior members.

---

## 2. Meetings, Learning & Growth
* **Active Participation**: Be attentive during meetings and actively participate.
* **Proactive Clarification**: If you do not understand a task or requirement, ask your team members or trainer for clarification.
* **Clear Direction First**: Before starting any work, make sure you clearly understand the requirements and direction.
* **Continuous Learning**: Learn from senior team members, accept guidance, and take initiative when needed.

---

## 3. Task Ownership & Time Management
* **End-to-End Ownership**: Take ownership of assigned tasks and follow them through to completion.
* **Effective Prioritization**: Prioritize tasks and manage your workload effectively.
* **Timelines & Deadlines**: Create and maintain a proper timeline for your tasks to avoid missing deadlines.
* **Early Communication of Delays**: Communicate early if you expect any delay in completing your work.

---

## 4. Communication & Team Collaboration
* **Regular Status Updates**: Keep the team updated about your progress, completed tasks, and blockers.
* **Discord & TODO Lists**: Provide regular updates in the Discord channel and maintain your TODO list.
* **Resource Sharing**: Share important project information such as GitHub project links, branch names, documentation links, and demo videos when required.
* **Holistic View**: Understand the overall project, not only your assigned tasks.
* **Team Awareness**: Ensure team members are aware of project progress and important updates.

---

## 5. Development & Quality
* **Pairing & Screen Sharing**: Share your screen when working with mentors or senior team members for better collaboration and guidance.
* **Thorough Testing**: Test your work thoroughly before submitting it for review.
* **Bug Notification Protocol**: If you find any bugs, incomplete tasks, or issues before testing by others, inform the team in the Discord channel with proper details, including the reason, current status, and possible solutions if available.
* **Publicizing Blockers**: Whenever you identify any issue or blocker, make sure to mention it in the appropriate channel so that everyone is aware and can take necessary actions.
* **Timeboxing Problems**: Timebox problems and ask for help if you are stuck instead of waiting too long.

---

## 6. Coding Standards & Git Practices
* **Meaningful Naming**: While coding, use clear and meaningful variable and function names that explain their purpose.
* **Clean Code & Dead Code Removal**: Before pushing code, clean up your implementation by removing unnecessary code, unused files, and unused imports.
* **Purposeful Comments**: Add proper comments in the code only when needed, such as explaining complex logic, future changes, debugging purposes, or important decisions.
* **Issue-Linked Commit Messages**: Use proper commit messages that include the relevant issue/task number.
* **Task-Related Branch Names**: Create meaningful branch names related to the current task or feature you are working on.
* **Atomic Commits**: Avoid pushing all changes in a single large commit. Make smaller, meaningful commits based on completed tasks or features.
* **Human Ownership of Git History**: Do not use AI to write your commit messages or give AI permission to commit and push code on your behalf. Write your own commit messages and review your code before pushing.
* **No Unverified AI Comments**: Do not add AI-generated comments to the code. If you do not understand the generated code or comments, do not keep them in the code.
* **Clean Folder Structure**: Use proper file names and maintain a clean and organized folder structure.
* **Secrets Management**: Never expose secret variables publicly. Store sensitive information such as API keys and credentials in `.env` files.
* **Pre-Push Code Review**: Before committing and pushing code, make sure you fully understand the changes and verify that the implementation is correct.

## 7. Documentation
* **Comprehensive Documentation**: Create and maintain proper documentation for your work and projects.
* **Continuous Updates**: Keep documentation updated whenever changes are made.

---

## 8. Attendance & Work Availability
* **Consistent Schedule**: Maintain proper check-in and check-out times every day. Everyone is expected to follow the official working schedule consistently.
* **Discord Availability**: Do not check out from Discord before completing your assigned tasks. If there is an emergency and you need to leave early, inform your senior team members, teammates, and the concerned supervisor.
* **Advance Absence Notice**: If you are unable to attend the office in person, inform your team members, supervisor, and relevant seniors at least one day in advance whenever possible.
* **WFH Attendance Tracking**: If you are working from home, make sure your check-in and check-out are properly recorded for that day.
* **WFH Responsiveness**: During work-from-home days, stay active and available on the Discord channel during office hours. Be responsive so that team members can communicate with you whenever needed.

---

## 9. Leave Process & Handover
* **Advance Notification**: Inform your team and trainer in advance if you will be absent, late, or unavailable.
* **Official Process**: Follow the leave process by submitting the required leave form and email.
* **Smooth Handover**: Properly hand over your tasks before taking leave so that other team members can continue the work smoothly.
* **Comprehensive Transfer**: Share the current progress, pending tasks, important files, and required instructions during the handover process.

---

## 10. Professional Work Environment & Security
* **Professional Communication**: Use Discord, email, and other communication platforms professionally.
* **Connection & Backup**: Maintain a stable internet connection and have a backup plan whenever possible.
* **Data Security & Confidentiality**: Keep company information secure and confidential.
